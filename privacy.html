Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

AdsPulse ("we", "us") is operated by Nilesh Singh, an individual based in Mumbai, India. AdsPulse is a reporting tool that shows advertising performance from Meta and Google Ads accounts that you choose to connect. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it and how you can delete it.

Data we access

Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads). When you connect Meta, we request the ads_read permission. This lets us read your ad account names, campaign, ad set and ad details, targeting settings, creative previews and performance insights. We also receive your Facebook name and user ID to show who is connected.

Google Ads. When you connect Google Ads, we request the Google Ads scope (adwords) plus your email address. This lets us read account, campaign, ad, keyword, search term and audience reports.

Information you give us. If you contact us or join the pilot, we keep your name, email and messages.

We do not request permission to create, change, pause or delete ads, and we do not access your personal Facebook or Google content beyond what is listed above.

How we use it

Only to display reports, analysis and recommendations to you inside AdsPulse, to operate and secure the service, and to respond to you. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the infrastructure providers that host the service on our behalf, or where required by law.

AdsPulse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted. Report data is cached only as long as needed to show your dashboards. Access to stored data is limited to the operator of AdsPulse.

Retention

We keep your connection and report data while your account is connected. When you disconnect a platform, its access token is deleted immediately and related cached data is removed within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect Meta or Google at any time from the Connections page, remove AdsPulse from your Facebook or Google account settings, or ask us to access, correct or delete your data. See our data deletion instructions.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify connected users.
