How it works

Setup takes a couple of minutes and doesn't need a developer.

  1. Connect

    Sign in with Facebook and Google and choose which ad accounts AdsPulse can read.

  2. Check

    AdsPulse pulls your performance data and compares every placement, creative and audience with your account's own averages.

  3. Act

    Open the alerts list, fix what's wasting money, and move budget to what's working.

Your data and access

You stay in control of your ad accounts at all times.

  • Read-only. AdsPulse asks only for permission to read reports (ads_read on Meta). It cannot create, edit, pause or delete ads.
  • Encrypted. Access tokens are stored encrypted and never shared.
  • Not sold, not shared. Your data is used only to show you your own reports.
  • Disconnect any time. One click removes access and deletes your stored token. See how to delete your data.
