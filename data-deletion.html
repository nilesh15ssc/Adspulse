Delete your data

Last updated: 5 October 2026

You can remove AdsPulse's access and delete your data at any time.

1. Disconnect inside AdsPulse

Open AdsPulse, go to Connections and click Disconnect next to Meta or Google Ads. The stored access token is deleted immediately and AdsPulse can no longer read that account.

2. Remove the app from Facebook or Google

Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → select AdsPulse → Remove.

Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select AdsPulse and choose Remove access.

3. Ask us to delete everything

Email nilesh15ssc@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email linked to your account, or include your Facebook name. We delete all data we hold about you within 30 days and confirm by email.
